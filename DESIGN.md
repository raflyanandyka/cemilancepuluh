## Brand & Style
This design system captures an artisanal, craveable, and welcoming identity tailored for direct-to-consumer gourmet snacks and local culinary gift boxes. It reconciles high-volume conversational commerce with premium gourmet aesthetics, tailored specifically for high-frequency purchasing contexts like Southeast Asian social commerce.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Appetizing & Comforting:** Warm, bakery-fresh, and buttery undertones replace sterile digital white backgrounds to evoke immediate sensory cravings.
- **Approachable & Trustworthy:** Friendly, communal, and direct. It minimizes friction with transparent cues, warm conversational triggers, and visible care.
- **Spirited & Crispy:** Energetic spicy and savory accents punctuate calm, creamy surfaces to trigger impulse and appetite.

### Visual Style Direction
The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Modernism** with **Conversational Tactility**:
- Soft, organic surfaces with rich roasted espresso text to eliminate visual fatigue.
- Generous, rounded surfaces (invoking smooth mochi, packaging pouches, and biscuits) offset by crisp, structured modular commerce grids.
- Integrated, frictionless WhatsApp order triggers that feel organic to the snack packaging visual language rather than utilitarian enterprise plugins.

## Colors
The palette evokes artisanal culinary warmth, freshness, and high-conversion conversational sales. Pure digital black (#000000) and sterile cool white (#FFFFFF) are strictly avoided.

### Role Assignments & Application
- **Primary (`#EA580C` / `#F97316`):** The signature snack glaze. Applied to high-priority calls to action, active checkout pathways, flash badges, and dynamic price highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1EBE5D` / `#25D366` / `#128C7E`):** Direct WhatsApp commerce channel. Dedicated strictly to chat checkouts, instant agent contact, confirmation toasts, and stock availability signals. Never dilute by using for arbitrary non-messaging interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Baked golden crust highlights. Used for loyalty status, promotional tags, review star scores, and bundle saver ribbons.
- **Neutral Text & Linework (`#2D1810` / `#3D2314`):** Dark roasted cocoa and espresso. Serves as primary body text, title headers, and subtle dividers, creating softer, richer contrast against cream backgrounds than harsh black.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas Surfaces:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FFFDF7` (Warm steamed rice / cream)
  - `Surface Muted / Cards`: `#FDF8ED` (Fresh butter cream)
  - `Surface Accent / Tonal Container`: `#F7EFE2` (Toasted biscuit)

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans was chosen as the singular typeface family across headlines, body, and UI metadata. Born out of Indonesian design heritage, it bridges geometric clarity with humanist warmth, providing impeccable legibility on small Android displays while maintaining organic charm.

### Localization & Copy Handling
- **Indonesian Syntax Adaptation:** Indonesian phrases typically expand word length by 15–25% compared to English (e.g., "Add to Cart" → "Tambah ke Keranjang", "Out of Stock" → "Stok Habis"). Every button, chip, and price label must support wrapping without truncating crucial pricing prefixes (`Rp`).
- **Currency & Numerals:** Prices use tabular numerals with weight `700` (`label-lg` or `title-md`) to ensure zero-staggered vertical alignment inside cart matrices and product cards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid-responsive responsive grid built around an 8px spatial rhythm, ensuring finger-friendly tap targets on mobile web and hybrid messaging viewports.

### Grid Specifications
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid grid. Gutter: `1rem` (16px), Canvas Margin: `1rem` (16px). Product listings default to 2 columns with aspect-ratio locked imagery to maximize scroll cadence.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid. Gutter: `1.25rem` (20px), Canvas Margin: `2rem` (32px).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at `1240px` max-width container. Gutter: `1.5rem` (24px), Canvas Margin: `3rem` (48px).

### Spacing Tokens
- `space-xs` (4px): Icon-to-text gaps, badge inner vertical padding.
- `space-sm` (8px): Metadata row spacing, input-to-label separation, chip gaps.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding, standard form field spacing, bottom bar offsets.
- `space-lg` (24px): Section title to grid offset, stacked product card margins.
- `space-xl` (40px): Full macro section gaps on mobile, landing page banner padding.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics tangible confectionery boxes, paper wraps, and stacked snack displays using warm-toned diffuse shadows instead of cold greys.

### Surface Tiers & Shadows
- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#FFFDF7` with no elevation.
- **Resting Product Cards (Tonal Layer + Soft Ambient Shadow):** Rendered on `#FDF8ED` with a 1px border of `rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.06)` and an appetizing warm drop shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.03)`
- **Hovered Cards & Interactive Trays:**
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.09), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.04)`
  - Subtle upward translation of `-2px`.
- **Floating Cart & Sticky WhatsApp Checkout Bar:**
  - `box-shadow: 0 -4px 20px 0 rgba(45, 24, 16, 0.08)`
  - Elevated above all scrolling surfaces to guarantee immediate conversion access on single-hand mobile usage.

## Shapes
The shape language takes inspiration from bakery items, soft biscuits, and pebble-like confections. Harsh rectangular geometry is systematically softened.

### Radius System
- **Pill Geometry (Full Rounded):** Applied to buttons, category filter tabs, status counters, and quantity steppers (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-2xl` equivalent):** Applied to product containers, promotional banners, and modal dialogs (`border-radius: 1.25rem` / 20px).
- **Form Controls & Micro Inputs (`rounded-lg` equivalent):** Applied to coupon input fields, review textareas, and shipping selector cards (`border-radius: 0.75rem` / 12px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Snack Action:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), background `#EA580C`, text `#FFFDF7`, font `label-lg`. Padding: `14px 24px`. On hover/active, transitions to `#C2410C`. Minimum touch height is 48px.
- **Conversational WhatsApp CTA:** Full pill radius, background `#25D366`, hover `#1EBE5D`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`. Features an inline WhatsApp SVG icon followed by direct localized text (e.g., "Pesan via WhatsApp").
- **Secondary Outline:** Cream surface `#FFFDF7`, border 1.5px solid `#2D1810`, text `#2D1810`.

### Cards (Product & Bundle)
- Surface `#FDF8ED`, border radius `1.25rem`, subtle outline `1px solid rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.06)`.
- Image container features an inner subtle beige vignette to soften photography edges.
- Floating dietary or flavor badges (e.g., "Pedas Gurih", "Halal", "Best Seller") anchor to the top-left with `0.5rem` offset.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Inactive state: `#F7EFE2` background, `#3D2314` text, no border.
- Active state: `#EA580C` background, `#FFFDF7` text, font weight `700`.
- Height: 36px, horizontal padding: 16px.

### Form Inputs & Checkouts
- Background: `#FFFDF7`, border: `1.5px solid rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.15)`. Text: `#2D1810`.
- Focus State: Border color `#EA580C` with a soft outer glow ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)`.
- Field padding: `12px 16px`, height: 48px.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkbox: `rounded-md` (6px radius). Checked state: `#EA580C` with white checkmark.
- Radio: Full circle. Selected: `#EA580C` dot inside `#FFFDF7` circle with `#EA580C` border.

### Snack-Specific Custom Components
- **Sticky Sticky Mobile Order Bar:** Glides into fixed bottom view containing real-time subtotal in `Rp` format alongside a split button: "Beli Sekarang" (Primary Orange) and a WhatsApp direct order icon button.
- **Flavor Profile Tag:** Micro-pills (`label-sm`) with soft spicy red, golden crunch amber, or herb green tint to indicate taste profiles at a glance.
